## Curation basis

- Full official podcast audio was resolved from the corresponding publisher RSS feeds and transcribed with dense English timestamps for curation.
- Exact YouTube source IDs and runtimes were independently resolved. Smart Friends and Naval-hosted sources share the same episode timebase to runtime-level rounding. Tim Ferriss #662 was cross-checked against Tim Ferriss's official speaker-labelled transcript; the RSS and YouTube editions contain the same conversation and differ only by a few seconds of overall padding/outro.
- Selection is intentionally conservative: Naval turns only, usually about 10–45 seconds, coherent thought boundaries, strong formulation/articulation value, minimal overlap, and reusable spoken structure.
- Guest-heavy exposition, interviewer turns, obvious backchannels, filler, and clips selected only for subject matter were excluded.
- Transcript text remains curation substrate only. No transcript tab or user-facing transcript corpus was added.

## Rule for future additions

A source is promoted only when the source identity, speaker, and start/end boundaries are defensible and the clip is genuinely useful for shadowing. Prefer fewer excellent clips over coverage for its own sake.
