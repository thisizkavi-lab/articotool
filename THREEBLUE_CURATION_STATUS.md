## Audit rules

1. Use the actual YouTube video ID and a timestamped caption/transcript source.
2. Inspect the text immediately before and after each proposed boundary; do not cut mid-thought just to hit a target length.
3. Prefer a complete explanatory move over a quotable sentence. Most clips should stay under ~60 seconds, but coherence wins.
4. Visual dependence is allowed; label the teaching move, not the mathematical claim.
5. Never promote a source from pending because a chapter marker, summary, memory, or approximate timestamp looks plausible.
6. Interviews, Q&As, podcasts, guest videos, and unrelated meta uploads stay out unless the corpus scope is deliberately changed.
